About Us

My sister was recently diagnosed with Celiac Diesease, an autoimmune disorder where eating gluten damages the small intestine.

Even 1/64th of a teaspoon of flour can cause intestinal damage. This makes it extremely hard for her to safely eat out at restaurants. Even when restaurants do advertise being “gluten free”, it has not always been to the level of safety she needs. VT Safe Bites allows individuals with celiac to trust restaurants based on other individuals’ feedback. The website allows local Vermonters with celiac to rate restaurants on their safety and accommodation for the disease.

Why is This Important?

Food brings people together, whether it is a celebration, a culture, or a shared bite. Due to this, celiac disease can be extremely isolating. The use of VT-Safe Bites helps individuals find a place where they don’t need to worry about their safety to enjoy connection and community.
