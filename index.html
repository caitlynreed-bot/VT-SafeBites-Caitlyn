VT Safe Bites: An Allergen Review

About Us: Welcome to VT Allergen Review!

My sister was recently diagnosed with Celiac Diesease which is an autoimmune disorder where eating gluten damages the small intestine.

Even 1/64th of a teaspoon flour can cause intestional damage. This makes it extremeley hard for her to safely eat out at restaurants.

VT Allergen Review allows local residents to rate the safety of restaurants on different allergens. Additionally, it provides a starting point for restaurants to improve their allergen safety.
